Sepsis Early Warning System

A dual-expert Mixture of Experts model that flags sepsis risk early from multivariate time-series vitals and explains its reasoning with an LLM.

01. Overview

A team project: a two-expert model that flags early sepsis risk from patient vitals and explains each prediction in plain language. My part was the time-series expert.

02. What I Did

  • Built the PatchTST + PyTorch prediction pipeline for multivariate, irregularly sampled vitals.
  • Prepared the patient data for time-series learning and handled class imbalance.
  • Integrated an LLM that writes an explanation alongside each risk score.
