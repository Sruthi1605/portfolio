WISE — Wealth Insights, Services & Enrollment

One app that takes a wealth-management client from opening an account online to being looked after by an advisor: WISE Onboarding (New Account Opening) and WISE Advisor 360.

Current · Individual project · Perficient 2026 Banking & Wealth Management
  • Java 21 · Spring Boot 3.5
  • Spring Security · JWT
  • PostgreSQL · Flyway
  • Spring Cache · Caffeine
  • Tesseract OCR · PDFBox
  • React · Chart.js
  • JUnit · Mockito · Postman

01. Overview

A wealth-management app covering the whole journey: a client opens an account online, Operations and Compliance review it, and once it's approved an advisor manages the client in Advisor 360. Advisory only — it never trades.

02. What I Built

  • 5 roles (Client, Operations, Compliance, Admin, Advisor) secured with Spring Security and JWT; the admin can never approve applications.
  • A 15-section onboarding form with backend validation (PAN, Aadhaar, IFSC, age, beneficiaries = 100%) and a risk profile.
  • Identity and bank numbers encrypted at rest and masked on screen; an OCR hint on uploaded IDs for reviewers.
  • Advisor 360: 3 plain-Java risk rules, a portfolio Health Score and suggestions with follow-ups.
  • Approval in one transaction that creates the account and assigns the least-busy advisor; cached dashboard totals.

03. How I Used AI

Built with AI coding assistants, with one rule: I had to understand every line before keeping it. I wrote the plan, reviewed each change and checked it with tests.
